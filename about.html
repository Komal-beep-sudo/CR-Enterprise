CR ENTERPRISE — CareRedefined® — CRENEU®

Who We Are

Trusted Quality, Redefined Care

CR ENTERPRISE, under the brand CareRedefined® — CRENEU®, is a fast emerging and growing company known as the Manufacturer, Importer, Exporter, Supplier & Distributor of trusted quality CSSD (Sterilization) products, Bio Medical Waste products, Lab products, Hospital Furniture, and Ortho, Ophthal, Neuro, Andrology, Dressing & Rehabilitation products.

Our products are made from the best quality raw materials and are highly appreciated in the market for their outstanding performance. They serve diverse application areas including hospitals, laboratories, blood banks, offices and homes — offering high durability, reliability, standard quality, ease of use, high tensile strength, safety and long operational life.

With advanced technology and skilled professionals, we ensure optimum quality products delivered on time. Every product is tested on multiple parameters for quality assurance before it is dispatched to the market.
